## Extensions & Additions

The operational layer and business automation features added in this repository include:
- **Javis Ops (`/ops`)**: Isolated web interface for customer support (Staff) and team managers (Manager) with strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).
- **Fanpage Care Engine**: Automated comment and Messenger ingestion, rules-first intent classification, draft generation, and human takeover protection.
- **TikTok Publishing**: PostPeer BYO key integration, 9:16 vertical video & photo carousel publishing with auto-music.
- **Multi-Brand CRM**: Unified lead, interaction, and customer profile management across multiple brand kits.
