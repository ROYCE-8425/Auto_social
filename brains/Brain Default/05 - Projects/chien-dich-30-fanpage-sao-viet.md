# Chien dich Dang Bai Tu Dong 30 Fanpage Sao Viet

Ke hoach van hanh dang bai tu dong moi ngay 1 bai tren he thong 30 Fanpage ve tinh va co so dao tao.

## 1. Nguyen tac van hanh da Fanpage

1. Moi Fanpage duoc phan bo 1 khung gio rieng (gian cach toi thieu 15 phut giua cac bai viet).
2. Xoay vong noi dung va nganh dao tao theo folder dataset:
   - Tin hoc van phong: `tin-hoc _ai`
   - Ke toan thuc hanh: `ke-toan`
   - Ve ky thuat AutoCAD / Co khi: `VE KY THUAT` hoac `co-khi`
   - Thiet ke do hoa: `do-hoa`
   - Ung dung AI van phong: `ai`
   - Marketing thuc chien: `marketing`
3. Anh Cover 1:1 duoc tao tu dong theo bo cuc Facebook 2026 hoac dung mau poster trong `_mau/`.
